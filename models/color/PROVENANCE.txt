Label order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet, purple, white, gray,
black, pink, brown, olive.
Input: float32 [batch,3,224,224], RGB ImageNet normalization, composite of
112x112 inner crop pasted at [56:168,56:168] into 224x224 wider crop.
The app adapts the source scripts/predict.py compose_input implementation.
Output: raw logits [batch,13], softmax distribution over those color labels.

This is a trained general color model, NOT a bottle-trained detector.
Its author trained on 28,860 procedural garment images and zero real photos.
Bottle color use is an experimental transfer; no bottle accuracy is claimed.
Confidence may be low or labels wrong under lighting/reflections/mixed colors.
The source model card documents ImageNet backbone and indoor-background
provenance. No training images or product evaluation photos are redistributed.
YOLOX remains responsible for detecting and tracking bottles. This network
only classifies the inner body crop of an already detected bottle.

Demo crop calibration: x .20-.80, y .18-.35 relative to the detected bottle
box. This samples the upper liquid shoulder and avoids the large printed
label. Selected after comparing fixed crops on three official Gatorade
product images (Cool Blue, Lemon Lime, Glacier Cherry). Those same examples
then predicted blue, yellow and white. This is calibration on three product
images, NOT independent accuracy validation or conveyor validation.
Images and exploratory evidence stay in ignored .build/color-model-research.
The actual trained weights are unchanged. No class remapping is applied.
